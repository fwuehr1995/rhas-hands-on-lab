= Cheatsheet

For your convenience, this page contains access information to all systems in your lab environment.

== Lab Login

There are no pre-provisioned accounts. You register your own account on the Red Hat Automotive Suite
at the start of the lab, and choose your own username and password.

TIP: Register in a *private browser window*. Cookies left over from another lab will sign you in with the wrong account.

NOTE: The email address you provide at registration is not verified.

== Environment checks

=== Check the image build service

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
caib status
----

=== Check your OpenShift identity

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
oc whoami
----

=== Authenticate to Jumpstarter

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
jmp-login
----

NOTE: Copy the printed URL with *Ctrl+Shift+C*, open it in a browser, and click *Yes* on the
*Grant Access to jumpstarter-cli* page.

== Jumpstarter Examples

=== List all Exporters

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
jmp get exporters
----

=== Lease an Exporter

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
jmp shell -l device=qemu
----

=== List hardware interfaces exposed on the Exporter

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
j
----

=== List available OS images

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
caib image list
----

=== Flash and boot a virtual device

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
DISKIMAGE=<uri from caib image list>
j flasher flash oci://$DISKIMAGE
----

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
j power on && j console start-console
----

=== Leave the serial console

Press *Ctrl+B* three times.

=== Get a shell in the qm partition

[source,sh,role=execute]
----
podman exec -it qm bash
----
